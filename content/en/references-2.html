Module 2 references

The profiles guia.eventos.v1 and guia.abertura.v1 are teaching recipes defined in the lessons, not Cardano standards. Consult the specifications and record the revision used before implementing an integration.

  • NIST FIPS 180-4: definition of SHA-256, used in the event, Merkle and opening examples.
  • RFC 8785: JSON Canonicalization Scheme. The limited serialization in Lesson 2.1 does not implement this entire scheme.
  • RFC 9162, section 2: Merkle trees, inclusion proofs and consistency proofs in the Certificate Transparency protocol. Lesson 2.2 uses the same prefixes for leaves and internal nodes in a four-file example; it does not implement the full protocol.
  • Cardano Developer Portal: fees: fee components and parameters. Lesson 2.3 combines a hypothetical event stream with the transaction measurement and parameters queried in Lesson 1.8.
  • Cardano Ledger: implementation and specifications for checking the rules of the era used in transaction calculations.
  • Hydra Head protocol: participants, commits, open state, snapshots, close, contestation and fanout.
  • Python: secrets: source of randomness used for the single-use nonce in Lesson 2.4.
  • LGPD and GDPR: official sources on purpose, minimization and protection of personal data. A cryptographic mechanism alone does not establish compliance.

This material supports technical learning. It does not replace current specifications, legal advice or a compliance assessment.
